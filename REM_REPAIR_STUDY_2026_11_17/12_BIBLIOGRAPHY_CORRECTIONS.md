Two-way citation check: every new entry must be cited in the text, and every in-text citation must have an entry. The check tool runs on the new edition's sources (`check_citations.py`), and its output is stored in the edition's audit folder. The tool checks identity and presence, not bibliographic correctness. Correctness is the purpose of the table above.
