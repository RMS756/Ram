## Interpretation
- **E1 + E2 recover legitimate work without any measured security cost** in every suite:
  - replay: +36 tasks primary, +166 dev2;
  - scripted: +1 benign task and +9 under attack;
  - generalisation: +8 authorised.
- **The gain is concentrated in the two design tasks.** The grouped interval therefore touches 0 (lower bound 0.0000). By the frozen rule this is **INCONCLUSIVE**, not GO (`14`).
- **The ledger shows no effect on the AgentDojo replay.** On the author-written diagnostics it prevents 16 unauthorised releases, and in the scripted loop it prevents one breach in one order. Its effect is real but confined to repeat and allowance situations that the recorded agents rarely produce.
- **Progent's manual policy has higher benign utility on this benchmark and a lower FP rate,** and it is the only arm that stops the ut15×it4 redirect in every order. It cannot express amounts and it refuses any account not on its hand-written list (fixtures 10/43, author-written suites 0).
- Neither C1 nor C1I is better than PG overall; they fail differently.
