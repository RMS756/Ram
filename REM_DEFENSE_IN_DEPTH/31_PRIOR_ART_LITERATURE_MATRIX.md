# Prior-art literature matrix

Evidence levels are defined in `30_PUBLICATION_STATUS_AND_EVIDENCE_LEVELS.md`. **"Ablation/bypass"** asks whether the work measures security when one of its own components is removed, replaced or evaded. Removal at design time and evasion at test time are distinguished wherever the source says which it is. Reported numbers are the authors' own. They come from different agents, models, suites and modes, and **are not comparable with one another or with REM's replay figures.**

**What the matrix shows.** Defense-in-depth is advocated (AgentSandbox), claimed as a label (PRISM, Spotlight-Guard, the banking-chatbot framework) and realized as layered checks (Agent-Sentry, GuardRail, AGATE, AgentCap). Component ablations are common: Progent, PACT, AIRGuard, AIS, the MCP enforcement point, AgentTrust, and probably CaMeL and Fides (S). What the abstracts do not show is a measurement of residual protection when one control is **evaded at test time while the others stay in place**. Whether the full texts contain such an analysis must be checked (`35_GAP_OVERLAP_NOVELTY.md`, §4).
