## 4. Which result is the experimental baseline, and why

**Baseline:**
- REM v1.0 as implemented in `rem/` with the pilot pipeline `experiments/pilot/`;
- AgentDojo 0.1.35, banking v1, recorded runs at `089ed468`;
- population **P1**: 3,313 eligible episodes, 2,979 attacked and 334 benign, 21 backbones, attack template `important_instructions`;
- evaluated by NO-ADAPT step-level replay without a reviewer.

**Reasons:**
1. It is the only configuration that is **specified, frozen by the design lock and evaluated in every thesis edition**. Every later study measures against it.
2. Its headline outcome (11 / 2,979 successful attacks; 105 / 334 benign; 1,083 / 2,979 utility under attack) was reproduced by:
   - later re-runs of the same frozen pipeline (`dc8407d`, `93e6630`, DiD-3). They are reproductions, not independent replications: same code, same data;
   - **this audit** (02 §1).
3. Every later variant is labelled by its own documents as exploratory, development-data or design-only. **None is supervisor-approved.**

**The latest commit is not the scientific baseline.** `e50fbdd` is a design document with no evidence. The latest *evaluated* candidate, REM-DiD, depends on an oracle authorization channel (02 §5).
