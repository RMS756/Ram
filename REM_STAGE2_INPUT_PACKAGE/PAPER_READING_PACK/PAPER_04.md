## What to read in the paper

**Reading mode:** Targeted reading.

Section and page numbers are given **only where a Stage 1 or Stage 2 record documents them**. Anything else is described by content, because the paper's own section numbering is not recorded.

- **Abstract.** Stage 1 verified the comparative-table row (Ch2-C048) at this level.
- **The method: the Actuarial Action Interface and the Authority Frontier.** Ch2 §2.6 (L87) describes both. That description has no claim-level audit record.

## Why this paper matters for understanding REM

Chapter 2 states it establishes that pricing an action's consequence before execution is not new. Chapter 3 lists it as prior art for consequence-priced control. It sets the boundary on what REM can claim about consequence tiers.
