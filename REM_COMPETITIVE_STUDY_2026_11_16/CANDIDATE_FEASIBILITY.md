The three implemented families that are new to this project are A (NP), C (GB) and D (CRC). They differ in kind: A and D replace the *decision criterion* on a fixed linear score by two different published calibration procedures (class-conditional error on steps; expected loss on episodes), and C replaces the *estimator* under a fixed decision criterion. GV, HVnc and HV4 are controls, not candidates.
