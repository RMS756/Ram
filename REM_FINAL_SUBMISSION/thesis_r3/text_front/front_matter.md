This document is a **draft for supervisor review** dated 14 November 2026 (third edition; it supersedes the second edition of the same date and the draft of 12 November 2026). It has not been approved and has not been submitted.

- **Basis.** It revises the five-chapter draft of 3 to 6 November 2026. Verified text was kept; new sections report the literature read in full on 11 November 2026, the rule baselines, the generalization and robustness analyses, and a confirmatory run. The second edition reorganizes Chapter 4 into six sections requested by the supervisor, adds an exploratory extension study with one published policy replayed in the same harness, and corrects several statements of the first edition. The third edition adds the repair of three defects of the extension's permission rule found by an external review, a call-level audit of the one benchmark task where all remaining attacks occur, and further corrections. The changes are listed in the change log that accompanies the thesis files.
- **Design status.** The design values (costs, consequence tiers, predicates, feature set and label rule) are declared values, not approved ones. REM v1.1 is a proposed amendment; REM v1.0 remains the design of record until the supervisor decides. The open decisions are listed in Appendix F.
- **Evidence.** All results come from recorded trajectories replayed under the assumption that the agent does not adapt. No live-agent experiment was executed, and no defense that needs a language model was run. One published policy, which needs no model, was replayed on the recorded episodes. The extension study is development evidence: it is proposed, exploratory and not adopted.
