**What this update changes.** The exploratory extension of this thesis releases a state-changing call only if its operation, recipient and amount match an instruction compiled from the request. This is close to CapLite's admission rule and to PACT's binding of authority-bearing arguments. ROPE's rule that values may come from the user's own records resembles the record check for refunds in Chapter 4 (Section 4.5.90). Request-derived checking of recipients and amounts, deterministic admission at the tool boundary, and financial specialization can therefore not be presented as unexplored. At abstract level, none of the three compares a learned decision placed behind such a check with the check used alone, which is the question the extension answers (Section 2.12.7). Their reported figures come from live agents and are not set beside REM's replay figures. Four further preprints that describe deterministic argument- or effect-level admission were found in the same searches and were not reviewed.
